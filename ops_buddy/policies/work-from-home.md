# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation of 3 months may work from home up to 2 days per week. Interns must work from the office.

## Childcare and family emergencies
Remote work is permitted for childcare emergencies and when a family member is unwell, for up to 5 extra days per quarter, with manager approval on the same day.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monitor for home use. Internet costs up to Rs 1,000 per month can be claimed under reimbursement code HR-204.
