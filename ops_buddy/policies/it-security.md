# IT and Security Policy

## Passwords and access
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days. Never share passwords, including with the IT team; IT will never ask for them. Access to production systems needs a ticket in the "access" category approved by your manager.

## Lost or stolen laptop
Report a lost or stolen laptop within 1 hour by opening a high-priority hardware ticket. IT will lock the device remotely. A police report is needed for replacement.

## Software installation
Only software from the company catalogue may be installed. For anything else, open a software ticket with the business reason. Personal VPNs are not allowed on company laptops.

## Ticket response times
High priority: response within 1 hour. Medium: within 1 working day. Low: within 3 working days.
