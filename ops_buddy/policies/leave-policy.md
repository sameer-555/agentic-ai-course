# Leave Policy

## Casual leave
Full-time employees get 8 casual leave days per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year. No more than 3 casual leave days may be taken in a row.

## Sick leave
Full-time employees get 8 sick leave days per year. For more than 2 sick days in a row, a medical certificate must be uploaded within 7 days of returning. Interns become eligible for sick leave after 30 days of joining.

## Earned leave
Employees earn 1.5 earned leave days per month worked. Up to 30 earned leave days can be carried forward. Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance, except in emergencies approved by the manager.

## Approval
Every leave request goes to the employee's reporting manager. Managers must respond within 2 working days. If a manager is on leave, the request goes to the skip-level manager.

## Notice period
During the notice period after resignation, earned leave cannot be used. Remaining earned leave is paid out in the final settlement.
