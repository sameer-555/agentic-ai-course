# Reimbursement Policy

## Medical reimbursement
Submit Form HR-204 for medical reimbursement, with original bills, within 30 days of the expense. The yearly limit is Rs 25,000 per employee.

## Travel claims
Submit Form HR-209 for travel claims within 15 days of returning. Economy class only for flights under 4 hours. Hotel limit is Rs 6,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 4,000 elsewhere.

## Internet and phone
Internet costs for working from home, up to Rs 1,000 per month, are claimed with Form HR-204 under the "home office" line.

## Payment timeline
Approved claims are paid with the next month's salary. Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
